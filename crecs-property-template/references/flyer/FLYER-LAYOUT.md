# Flyer layout

Generated by the CRE Cloud Solutions plugin (`tools/flyer-layout/build-flyer-reference.php`) from its own registry. Do not edit by hand: regenerate. The machine-readable form, which `scripts/crecs_flyer.py` reads, is `flyer-layout.json` beside this file.

The property flyer's designed pages are drawn by the plugin from a **flyer layout document**: a tree of blocks in Elementor's shape that says what each page holds, where, and how it looks. CRE Cloud prints the pages to PDF and adds the property's attachment pages after them. The data blocks print only the property's own data, escaped; nothing in a document is free markup or CSS, so a design can arrange, restyle and retext the blocks and never rewrite them.

## The document

```json
{"type": "crecs-flyer-layout", "version": 1, "title": "…", "content": [ <one crecs_flyer block> ]}
```

Every block is `{"id", "elType", "widgetType", "settings", "elements"}`. `id` is 4 to 16 letters or digits, unique in the document. `elType` is `container` for blocks that hold others and `widget` for the rest. `widgetType` names the block, for containers too. `settings` holds only the controls listed below for that block; `elements` is the list of blocks inside. `title` is optional: one line of at most 120 characters.

The tree has a fixed shape:

- the **Flyer** (`crecs_flyer`), the root, once;
  - the **Header band** (`crecs_flyer_header`) and the **Footer band** (`crecs_flyer_footer`), once each, required;
  - **Pages** (`crecs_flyer_page`), 1 to 4;
    - bands and pages hold **rows** (`crecs_flyer_row`), which hold **columns** (`crecs_flyer_column`), which hold the content blocks, and rows again.

A document holds exactly `type`, `version`, `title` (optional) and `content`, and every block exactly its five keys: nothing else, not even Elementor's own (`isInner`, `page_settings`…).

Limits: at most 150 blocks, nested at most 8 deep (the flyer counts as 1, a page or band 2, its row 3, its column 4, a row inside that column 5, its column 6, a row inside that 7, its column 8). So a column can hold a row whose columns hold blocks, or another row; that second nested row's columns are at the limit and must stay empty.

A design has at most 2 **Logo** blocks (`crecs_flyer_logo`): the site logo and a media library image each carry a whole embedded picture, and a band prints it again on every page and in both attachment frames, so more would make the request to CRE Cloud too large. The limit counts every Logo block, the two logos kept in CRE Cloud (`company_logo`, `flyer_logo`) included. The error is E-FL-LIMIT.

## The page

One page box is exactly one US Letter page: landscape is 1056 x 816 pixels, portrait 816 x 1056 (96 pixels to the inch). The flyer's `orientation` sets the designed pages'; each attachment page keeps its own orientation.

- The header and footer bands print at the top and bottom of every designed page (a page can hide either) and of the frames around attachments. Their height is fixed (`height` on each band).
- A page's body is what is left between the bands. Its height is fixed and what does not fit in it is cut off: keep a page's content within it.
- Attachment frames are generated, not designed: the header band, the attachment, the footer band, one frame per orientation.
- Rows lay their columns side by side and are printed as tables (the PDF printer has no flex or grid). A column's `width` is a share of its row in percent; columns without one share what the others leave.

Sizes that must add up (the plugin refuses a design where they do not):

- **A page's body keeps at least 100 pixels of a landscape page's 816.** The header and footer heights, and the padding and borders of the flyer (`page_…`) and of the page's own body (`body_…`), all come out of 816 pixels, on every page and whatever the orientation (a band's own padding and border, `band_…`, are inside its height and take nothing more): the frames around attachments are printed in landscape too, with both bands, so a portrait design must fit a landscape page as well, and a page that hides a band still counts it.
- **A fixed height keeps at least 10 pixels for what it holds.** A fixed height is the outer size of the part it sizes: that part's padding and border, top and bottom, are inside it and come out of it: a band's `band_…`, a row's `row_…` (when its `height` is not `auto`), a photo's or a photo grid's `photo_…`, the map's `map_…`, the logo's `logo_…` and a contact photo's `photo_…` (a contact's `photo_size` is the photo's outer width as well). Each option below says which.
- **A row's columns fit on the narrowest page.** Each column needs at least 40 pixels: a row's columns at 40 pixels, with its `gap` between each two, must fit in a portrait page's 816 pixels.

Fonts: `Lato`, `DejaVu Sans`, `DejaVu Serif`, `Nimbus Sans L`, `Nimbus Roman No9 L`, `URW Gothic L`, `URW Bookman L`, `URW Palladio L`, `Century Schoolbook L`. These are the fonts the flyer printer has; no other font can be used, because nothing else is loaded.

## What the plugin refuses

The preview and sending refuse a document with any of these.

| code | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `E-FL-SHAPE` | not a flyer layout document: `type` "crecs-flyer-layout", `version` 1, a `content` list and, when given, a `title` of one line of at most 120 characters; nothing else |
| `E-FL-ROOT` | `content` is not exactly one `crecs_flyer` block, or one sits inside another block |
| `E-FL-REQUIRED` | the header band or the footer band is missing |
| `E-FL-ONCE` | a block that appears once (the flyer, the header band, the footer band) appears more often |
| `E-FL-PAGES` | no page, or more than 4 |
| `E-FL-LIMIT` | more than 150 blocks, or nested more than 8 deep, or more than 2 logo blocks, whatever their source (each prints again on every page of its band and in each attachment frame, and a site logo or a media library image carries a whole picture) |
| `E-FL-NODE` | a block without its `id`, `elType`, `widgetType`, `settings` and `elements`, or with any other key |
| `E-FL-ID` | an id that is not 4 to 16 letters or digits, or that two blocks share |
| `E-FL-BLOCK` | a `widgetType` the flyer does not have |
| `E-FL-ELTYPE` | an `elType` that is not the block's kind (`container` or `widget`) |
| `E-FL-CHILDREN` | a widget holding blocks |
| `E-FL-WITHIN` | a block directly inside a parent it does not allow |
| `E-FL-SETTING` | a setting the block does not have |
| `E-FL-VALUE` | a value outside its control's shape or range, or settings that do not go together: a row's column widths, a row's columns and gaps against the narrowest page, a logo's source and image, a map's boundaries without a satellite or hybrid map, a fixed height against the padding and border inside it, a page's room between the bands |
| `E-FL-FONT` | a font the flyer printer does not have |

## Setting names

- `{target}_{property}`: the style of a named part of a block (each block lists its parts below). A flyer is printed: there is no hover state and no device, so a setting has one value.
- `text_{slot}`: one of the block's fixed texts, printed as plain text. Unset, the block's own text (shown below).
- `{option}`: what the block shows rather than how it looks (each block lists its options below, with what they take and their value while unset).

An empty value (`""`, `null`, an empty list, a size of `""`, four empty sides) means "as the block has it".

### Style properties

A part's kinds say which properties it takes: `box` (a box the design sizes), `surface` (a box whose size the block sets itself, through an option or the page box: no `height` or `width`) and `text`.

| property | kinds | value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `background_color` | box, surface | a colour: `#rgb`, `#rrggbb`, `rgb()`, `rgba()` or `transparent` (not `#rgba` or `#rrggbbaa`: the flyer printer ignores a hex colour with transparency, so write it `rgba()`) |
| `padding` | box, surface | `{"unit": "px", "top": n, "right": n, "bottom": n, "left": n}` (and, if you like, `"isLinked"`: true or false) and no other key, each n a number from 0 to 100 with at most 3 decimals |
| `border_width` | box, surface | `{"unit": "px", "top": n, "right": n, "bottom": n, "left": n}` (and, if you like, `"isLinked"`: true or false) and no other key, each n a number from 0 to 20 with at most 3 decimals |
| `border_color` | box, surface | a colour: `#rgb`, `#rrggbb`, `rgb()`, `rgba()` or `transparent` (not `#rgba` or `#rrggbbaa`: the flyer printer ignores a hex colour with transparency, so write it `rgba()`) |
| `border_radius` | box, surface | `{"unit": "px", "top": n, "right": n, "bottom": n, "left": n}` (and, if you like, `"isLinked"`: true or false) and no other key, each n a number from 0 to 200 with at most 3 decimals |
| `height` | box | `{"unit": u, "size": n}` and no other key, u one of `px`, n a number from 0 to 1056 with at most 3 decimals |
| `width` | box | `{"unit": u, "size": n}` and no other key, u one of `px`, `%`, n a number from 0 to 1056 with at most 3 decimals (at most 100 in `%`) |
| `color` | text | a colour: `#rgb`, `#rrggbb`, `rgb()`, `rgba()` or `transparent` (not `#rgba` or `#rrggbbaa`: the flyer printer ignores a hex colour with transparency, so write it `rgba()`) |
| `font_family` | text | one of the flyer printer's fonts (below) |
| `font_size` | text | `{"unit": u, "size": n}` and no other key, u one of `px`, n a number from 6 to 96 with at most 3 decimals |
| `font_weight` | text | one of `300`, `400`, `500`, `600`, `700`, `800`, `900` |
| `text_transform` | text | one of `none`, `uppercase`, `lowercase`, `capitalize` |
| `letter_spacing` | text | `{"unit": u, "size": n}` and no other key, u one of `px`, n a number from -5 to 20 with at most 3 decimals |
| `line_height` | text | `{"unit": u, "size": n}` and no other key, u one of `""` (no unit: a multiple of the font size), `px`, n a number from 0 to 200 with at most 3 decimals |
| `text_align` | text | one of `left`, `center`, `right`, `justify` |

### Map — `crecs_flyer_map`

Widget, may repeat; goes directly in the column (`crecs_flyer_column`).

The property's map, as wide as its column and `height` tall. With `map_type` `road` (the default) it is the road map CRE Cloud keeps for the property. With `satellite` or `hybrid` CRE Cloud makes an aerial picture in the same box when the flyer is printed, optionally with the property's boundaries (`boundaries` `on`) and at a chosen `zoom`; for example `{"map_type": "hybrid", "boundaries": "on", "zoom": 17, "height": 300}`. A property that hides its address shows no map of any kind, aerial or road, and neither does a property with no location: the block then prints nothing.

Parts you can style:

- `map`, the block itself (surface): `map_background_color`, `map_padding`, `map_border_width`, `map_border_color`, `map_border_radius`

Options:

- `height`: the map's height in pixels; its width is the column's. For a satellite or hybrid map the two together are the shape of the picture CRE Cloud makes. Value: a whole number from 60 to 1056 pixels. Unset: `300`. It is the outer size of `map`. The padding and border of `map`, top and bottom, come out of it and must leave at least 10 pixels.
- `map_type`: the kind of map: `road` is the road map CRE Cloud keeps for the property, `satellite` an aerial photograph and `hybrid` an aerial photograph with the road names on it. CRE Cloud makes the two aerial kinds itself when the flyer is printed, so they need CRE Cloud to support them: check the session's capabilities first (`flyer_map_types` lists the map types CRE Cloud makes, and is null when that is not known), offer `satellite` and `hybrid` only when they are listed, and keep `road` when they are not. When CRE Cloud cannot make an aerial map it prints the property's road map instead, if it has one, and says so in the print's warnings. Value: one of `road`, `satellite`, `hybrid`. Unset: `road`.
- `boundaries`: `on` also draws the property's boundaries and overlays, which are what has been drawn on the property in CRE Cloud: nothing extra appears if nothing is drawn there. Only a `satellite` or `hybrid` map can show them, so `on` with a `road` map is refused (E-FL-VALUE). Value: one of `off`, `on`. Unset: `off`.
- `zoom`: how close an aerial map is, from 1 (the most land) to 21 (the closest); a `road` map has its own zoom and ignores this. CRE Cloud always takes about 640 pixels across at that zoom and then draws the picture at the block's size, so the same zoom shows the same area whatever the block's size, and a smaller zoom number shows more land; to show more or less land change the zoom, not the block's size. Unset, the zoom is the property's own zoom level in CRE Cloud (16 when it has none). Value: a whole number from 1 to 21.
